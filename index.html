OUR PRODUCTS

Purpose-Built Solutions. Complete Control.

Whether you need to track and manage critical keys across facilities or enable secure storage of IT assets within a corporate campus, Meclix has the answer.

KeyKnox — Electronic Key Management System

India's most advanced electronic key cabinet system. Biometric authentication, real-time tracking, and comprehensive audit logs for every key in your organisation. Scales from a single cabinet to enterprise-wide deployments.

  • Individually locked smart key slots with positional tracking
  • Dual-factor authentication (Biometric fingerprint + RFID card)
  • Automatic SMS & email alerts for overdue key returns
Learn More about KeyKnox

ArcaKnox — Smart Locker Infrastructure

Enterprise-grade smart locker infrastructure for Employees, IT Assets, Visitors, and Tool Management. Customisable to your workflows — from reception desk operations to manufacturing shop floors.

  • Space-saving modular configuration expanding vertically
  • Integrated device charging hubs (optional USB configuration)
  • RFID, Biometric fingerprint, PIN, and mobile app access
Learn More about ArcaKnox

Unified Management Platform
