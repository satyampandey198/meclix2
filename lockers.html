ArcaKnox Smart Locker

Smart Lockers for Every Use Case

Meclix's ArcaKnox gives organisations total control over asset storage, assignment, and retrieval — with real-time tracking, contactless access, and tamper-proof audit logs. Purpose-built for demanding enterprise environments.

Space-Smart by Design

Vertical Efficiency, Horizontal Freedom

Floor space is the most expensive asset in any facility — every metre given to storage is a metre taken away from operations, movement, and productivity. Meclix's ArcaKnox (Smart Locker) is designed to work upward, not outward.

ArcaKnox configurations expand vertically, making full use of available wall height while keeping the horizontal footprint to a minimum. Stacked intelligently from base to ceiling, this is particularly valuable in high-density environments like IT server rooms, factory floors, reception lobbies, and office corridors.

The result is a locker deployment that fits neatly against walls and gaps, occupies minimal floor real estate, and scales upward as your needs grow — without ever getting in the way of operations.

Employee Asset Lockers

EMPLOYEE ASSETS — Safe, Managed, Accountable

Modern workplaces are characterized by dynamic seating and hot-desking, alongside shared co-working models. This requires secure, flexible lockers for employees. ArcaKnox replaces manual key allocation with instant card or biometric access.

Key Benefits
  • Dynamic assignment: Allocate and reassign from the dashboard without manual coordination.
  • Multi-authorize access: Biometric, RFID card, or Mobile App configurations.
  • Shift-aware scheduling: Prepares and releases lockers automatically at shift boundaries.
  • Full usage history: Audit-ready logs for compliance reporting and HR reviews.
  • Instant alerts: Tamper and forced-entry notifications sent to security.

IT Asset Lockers

IT ASSETS — Track Laptops, Devices, and Peripherals

Valuable IT assets are frequently misplaced. ArcaKnox IT Asset Lockers provide a fully audited, self-service system for secure checkout and return of laptops, tablets, access tokens, and storage devices. Eliminates spreadsheet tracking and reduces losses.

Key Benefits
  • Compartment-level tracking: Every device checkout is logged with timestamp and ID.
  • ITSM Integration: Links with asset software to update CMDB records.
  • Integrated Charging: charging-enabled slots keep devices powered between shifts.
  • Overdue alerts: Notifications when devices are not returned within the window.
  • Temporary access: Time-bounded credential provisioning for contractors.
IT Team Workflows
1. Verify & Access

IT Admin grants role-based permissions or logs visitor ticket. Employee scans card to open compartment.

2. Always Charged

Device charges inside locker via integrated USB/power distribution unit.

3. Asset Log Sync

System syncs automatically with ITSM platform database on return.

Target Industries
IT Parks Data Centres Corporate HQs Universities Banking & BFSI
Visitor Locker Infrastructure

VISITOR ASSETS — Secure Belongings. Elevate Experience.

Improve building security and reduce front-desk workload in areas where guests must store laptops, phones, or luggage. ArcaKnox links locker allocation directly with visitor management systems (VMS).

Key Benefits
  • Linked checkout: Locker assignments linked directly to QR codes or badges.
  • Self-service operations: Visitors open lockers without receptionist support.
  • Auto-release rules: Cabinets open and reset when a visitor badge is scanned for exit.
  • Compact locker design: Fits perfectly in main reception and office lobbies.
Visitor Journey
1. QR Code Issuance

Visitor checks in at VMS kiosk and receives a printout or digital QR credential.

2. Instant Allocation

Scan QR at ArcaKnox Point terminal. Locker opens automatically. Belongings are stored securely.

3. Auto Release

Visitor returns, retrieves assets, and logs out. System clears and opens locker slot for the next guest.

Target Industries
Corporate Campuses Government Offices Defence Sites Data Centres Refineries
Tool Kit Lockers

TOOL KITS — Reduced Loss. Full Shift Accountability.

In manufacturing, maintenance, and utility environments, tool kits represent high-value assets and safety concerns. ArcaKnox enforces a digital chain of custody, ensuring that no tool is left untracked on the factory floor.

Key Benefits
  • Kit-level accountability: Track checkout and returns mapped to technician ID.
  • Role-based restrictions: Limit access to high-risk tools based on training levels.
  • Damaged item logging: Technicians report issues directly at the HMI screen.
  • Shift-end enforcement: Auto alerts to supervisors if critical tools are not returned.
