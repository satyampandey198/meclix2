About Meclix Mechatronix

Access Accountability, Without Compromise

"We believe every organisation and every stakeholder in the organisation deserves complete confidence in who has access to what — and when. No guesswork. No manual logs. No missing assets & no missing keys."

Meclix Mechatronix was founded with one conviction: that the physical access layer — keys, and assets — is the most under-managed security surface in all enterprises. We set out to change that scenario with technology that is rigorous, dependable, intelligent, and genuinely easy to deploy.

Our two core product lines, KeyKnox (Electronic Key Management System) and ArcaKnox (Smart Locker), are designed from the ground up for critical operations — with support for compliant reporting, integration with relevant third-party platforms, and field-proven hardware suited to all power and climate conditions.

Engineered for high reliability, and backed by extensive experience in identity systems and electronic security.

Standards

Industry Best Practices

Our design processes incorporate standardized operations to meet modern audit compliance requirements.

KeyKnox — Key Best Practices

  • Key Lifecycle Management

    Defines clear processes for key issuance, usage, return, and deactivation to ensure full lifecycle control of physical keys.

  • Segregation of Duties

    Ensures separation between administrators and users to reduce risk and enforce operational governance.

  • Compliance-Ready Reporting

    Provides structured reporting aligned with audit and compliance requirements, supporting internal and external audits.

  • Exception-Based Monitoring

    Flags unusual access patterns or deviations from defined policies to enable proactive security intervention.

  • Credential Management Standardization

    Supports consistent use of identity credentials across systems, reducing duplication and improving access control governance.

  • Centralised Policy Enforcement

    Enables uniform access rules and policies to be applied across all cabinets and locations from a central platform.

ArcaKnox — Locker Best Practices

  • Demand-Based Locker Allocation

    Allocates lockers based on real-time demand and usage patterns to maximise utilisation efficiency.

  • Usage-Based Access Policies

    Defines locker access rules based on time, user type, and operational requirements to ensure controlled usage.

  • Integration with Workplace Ecosystem

    Ensures seamless coexistence with systems such as visitor management, access control, and workplace applications.

  • Resource Utilisation Optimisation

    Continuously monitors occupancy and usage trends to reduce idle capacity and improve infrastructure planning.

  • User Self-Service Enablement

    Empowers users to independently access, release, and manage locker usage, reducing dependency on administrative support.

  • Standardised Configuration Management

    Ensures consistent locker configurations and policies across multiple locations and deployments.
