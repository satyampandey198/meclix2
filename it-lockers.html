ArcaKnox Smart Locker

Smart Lockers for Every Use Case

Meclix's ArcaKnox gives organisations total control over asset storage, assignment, and retrieval — with real-time tracking, contactless access, and tamper-proof audit logs. Purpose-built for demanding enterprise environments.

Space-Smart by Design

Vertical Efficiency, Horizontal Freedom

Floor space is the most expensive asset in any facility — every metre given to storage is a metre taken away from operations, movement, and productivity. Meclix's ArcaKnox (Smart Locker) is designed to work upward, not outward.

ArcaKnox configurations expand vertically, making full use of available wall height while keeping the horizontal footprint to a minimum. Stacked intelligently from base to ceiling, this is particularly valuable in high-density environments like IT server rooms, factory floors, reception lobbies, and office corridors.

The result is a locker deployment that fits neatly against walls and gaps, occupies minimal floor real estate, and scales upward as your needs grow — without ever getting in the way of operations.

IT Asset Lockers

IT ASSETS — Track Laptops, Devices, and Peripherals

Valuable IT assets are frequently misplaced. ArcaKnox IT Asset Lockers provide a fully audited, self-service system for secure checkout and return of laptops, tablets, access tokens, and storage devices. Eliminates spreadsheet tracking and reduces losses.

Key Benefits
  • Compartment-level tracking: Every device checkout is logged with timestamp and ID.
  • ITSM Integration: Links with asset software to update CMDB records.
  • Integrated Charging: charging-enabled slots keep devices powered between shifts.
  • Overdue alerts: Notifications when devices are not returned within the window.
  • Temporary access: Time-bounded credential provisioning for contractors.
IT Team Workflows
1. Verify & Access

IT Admin grants role-based permissions or logs visitor ticket. Employee scans card to open compartment.

2. Always Charged

Device charges inside locker via integrated USB/power distribution unit.

3. Asset Log Sync

System syncs automatically with ITSM platform database on return.
